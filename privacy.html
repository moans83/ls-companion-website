Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Gruvi is a privacy-first women's health and wellbeing app designed to help you understand your body, navigate changing health needs, and feel more in control of your wellbeing. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible while still providing meaningful insights.

1. Information You Provide

You may choose to enter information such as:

  • Symptoms
  • Self-check entries
  • Notes
  • Triggers and care activities

2. Account Information (Optional)

You can use Gruvi without creating an account.

If you choose to sign in using Apple, we may store a unique identifier and (optionally) your email address if you choose to share it.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

Your data is securely stored using Firebase (Google Cloud services).

If you are signed in, your data is associated with your account so it can be accessed across devices.

If you are not signed in, your data may only be stored locally on your device.

3. Anonymous Community Participation

If you choose to participate in community features such as answering community questions or leaving provider notes, Gruvi stores a pseudonymous device identifier.

This identifier is used to:

  • Prevent duplicate answers and notes from the same device
  • Allow you to update your own answers and notes later
  • Generate aggregated community insights

This identifier is not used to directly identify you and is not shared with other users.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide symptom tracking and insights
  • Help you identify patterns over time
  • Improve the app experience
  • Prevent duplicate community submissions and maintain community features

5. Use of Anonymized Data for Insights

To generate summaries and insights, journal logs, notes, and aggregated community responses may be processed using AI-based services, including models provided by Google and Anthropic.

We take steps to minimize personal information sent to these services. Community insights are generated using aggregated responses and are not intended to identify individual users.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We use trusted third-party services (such as Firebase) to securely store and process data on our behalf.

Community answers and provider notes may be displayed to other users in anonymized form.

7. Your Choices

  • Use the app without signing in
  • Delete the app at any time
  • Request deletion of your account data

8. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the app’s functionality.

9. Children’s Privacy

Gruvi is not specifically designed for children. If you are under the age of majority in your region, please use the app with guidance from a parent or guardian.

10. Analytics

We collect limited analytics information to understand app usage, improve features, measure performance, and identify issues. Analytics data is not intended to directly identify you.
