A private lichen sclerosus tracker for symptoms, flares, treatment, and self-checks

LS Companion is designed to help people with lichen sclerosus track symptoms like itching, burning, pain, tearing, flare-ups, treatment use, and self-check observations over time. Keeping a simple record can make it easier to notice patterns, remember changes, and communicate more clearly with a healthcare provider.

Track vulvar lichen sclerosus changes over time

Use LS Companion as a lichen sclerosus journal to record daily logs, flare patterns, self-check notes, treatment reminders, and appointment prep. The app is built for private tracking and does not require a login for local use.

LS Companion is for tracking and educational purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always speak with a qualified healthcare provider about medical concerns or treatment decisions.
