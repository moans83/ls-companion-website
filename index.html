Private by design

LS Companion is built for personal health tracking. You can use the app locally without creating an account, giving you a simple way to keep track of sensitive health information privately.

Track perimenopause and hormonal changes alongside everything else

Perimenopause can affect much more than your period. Use LS Companion to keep track of cycle changes, sleep, mood, energy, hot flashes, vulvar and vaginal symptoms, libido, wellbeing and other changes — while seeing them alongside the rest of your health.

Your health doesn't always fit into one box

Symptoms can overlap. Hormones can change. Sleep, stress, cycles, treatments and vulvar health may all be part of the picture. LS Companion gives you one place to keep track of what you're experiencing and see how things change over time.

LS Companion is for personal health tracking and educational purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always speak with a qualified healthcare provider about medical concerns, symptoms or treatment decisions.
